Privacy Policy — Ten Drop

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Ten Drop ("the Game"), developed by NikStudio ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. The Game keeps your progress and settings (best score, daily streak, sound, music and vibration settings) only on your device. This data is not sent to us.

Advertising

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device model and operating system, and information about how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's pause menu ("Privacy"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
